A tracked robot that tidies a room by itself

I’m building an autonomous ground robot that finds objects on the floor and puts them where they belong: socks to the laundry basket, toys to the toy box. Jetson Orin Nano for vision, ESP32 for motor control, ROS2 tying it together.

Stage 1

Drive platform

TS100 metal tank chassis, encoder motors, ESP32 + L298N, wireless controller.

Working
Stage 2

Vision and autonomy

IMX219 camera on the Jetson, object detection, ROS2 nodes, battery power.

In progress
Stage 3

Arm and pick-up

SO-101 5-axis arm, grasping detected objects and placing them by category.

Planned

System architecture

Compute
NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano, developed headless over SSH
Middleware
ROS2
Vision
Sony IMX219 over CSI
Motor control
ESP32, L298N H-bridge, quadrature encoders
Chassis
TS100 metal tank, 2× 25 mm 9–12 V gear motors
Manipulator
SO-101 5-axis arm
Power
3S LiPo, fully untethered
Electronics
Custom PCB (in design)
