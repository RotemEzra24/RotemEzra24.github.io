Robotics project

An autonomous tracked robot that tidies a room

The goal: a robot that finds objects on the floor and puts each one where it belongs, socks in the laundry basket and toys in the toy box. I am building it in stages, each layer working before the next goes on top: an ESP32 drives the tracks, and an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano handles vision.

Build log

Built in stages so every layer works before the next one goes on top.

Step 1

Assembling the chassis

The base is a TS100 metal tank chassis kit. I chose a ready-made chassis so the effort goes into the system rather than the mechanics, and assembled it from the kit parts.

  • Metal frame plates, wheels, standoffs and screws from the kit
  • Two 25 mm gear motors with built-in encoders, labelled DC 9 V, 120 RPM
Step 2

Chassis complete

The assembled chassis before any electronics: two tracks with skid steering, and the two gear motors with their encoders at the rear.

  • Tracked platform, steered by driving the two tracks independently
  • Encoder boards on both motors, ready to be wired to the controller
Step 3

Drive electronics on a breadboard

I prototyped the drive electronics on a breadboard before soldering anything: an ESP32 dev board wired through jumper wires to an L298N dual H-bridge on the chassis, with both motor encoders connected back to the ESP32.

  • ESP32 controls both motors through the L298N
  • Encoder feedback read by the ESP32 and used in the drive code
  • Driven manually with a Sony game controller via the ESP32
  • Powered from a 12 V wall adapter
Step 4

Soldered wiring, Jetson and camera

With the drive working, I replaced the breadboard with soldered wiring and a screw-terminal breakout for the ESP32. Then I added the compute layer: a Jetson Orin Nano on a raised clear acrylic deck on brass standoffs, with the L298N underneath and the IMX219 camera in an acrylic holder at the front.

  • IMX219 camera connected to the Jetson over CSI
  • Jetson set up headless, developed from my MacBook over SSH / VS Code
  • YOLO models loaded onto the Jetson for object detection

What’s next

Vision and autonomy

Object detection with YOLO on the camera feed, the ROS2 software on the Jetson, and a link from the Jetson to the ESP32 so the robot can drive itself.

Power and PCB

Move from the 12 V wall adapter to a 3S LiPo (already ordered) so the robot is fully untethered, then replace the hand wiring with a custom PCB.

Arm and pick-up

Add the SO-101 5-axis arm and close the loop: detect an object, drive to it, pick it up and place it where it belongs.
